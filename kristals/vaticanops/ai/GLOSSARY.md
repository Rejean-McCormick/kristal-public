# Glossary

- **Kristal:** locally authored versioned body of semantic/operational descriptions.
- **VaticanOps:** editorial project, not the Vatican.
- **Notitia de delicto:** information about a presumed delict, treated as information rather than proven guilt.
- **DDF:** Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith.
- **Sede vacante:** vacancy of the Holy See.
- **Condition/guard:** descriptive logical branch whose applicability requires interpretation, not machine authorization.
